    Settings
    Change anything, test it, then click Save. Everything stays on this computer.
    
  
    The settings file could not be read, so these boxes show the defaults. Saving replaces it.
    

  
    
  
    
    
    Gemini key Needed
    
    
    Gemini is Google's AI. It reads each video's captions and writes the digest. The key is free:
      open Google AI Studio, click Create API key, copy it and paste it here.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    YouTube channels Needed
    
    
    The channels whose new videos go into the digest. Click a popular one, or add your own: open the channel on YouTube and copy
      the address from the top of the browser, like https://www.youtube.com/@TheStreet, or type its handle, like @TheStreet.
    
    
    Popular channels (click to add):
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Analysts to follow Optional
    
    
    Want only certain people? Pick them, and only videos where they speak go into the digest.
      Leave the list empty to include every video from your channels.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    Write the full name, like Dan Ives. If captions spell it differently, add the other spelling after a slash: Tom Lee / Thomas Lee.
    
    
    
      
    Suggestions (click to add; a number is how many of your saved videos they are in):
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    For example interviews about politics or sport, with no stocks, market levels or predictions in them.
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Your stocks Optional
    
    
    The stocks you watch get a price table in each digest and on the dashboard, next to what the analysts said about them.
      Add how many shares you own to see each one's weight in your holdings. Prices are fetched by this computer; no sheet or account is needed,
      and your share counts never leave this computer.
    
    
    TickerShares you own (optional)
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    How you get the digest
    
    
    Every digest is kept in Market Digest, under Home > Recent digests. Choose how you hear about a new one.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    
      
    
        
        
    
        
    
          
          
    
        
    
        
    The digest is sent by the Market Digest email service. The code proves the address is yours, so no one else can send mail to it.
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    Email needs a Gmail account to send from, with an app password (two minutes to set up; the link is below). Skip this if the notice is enough.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
      
      
    
      
    
      
    Not your normal Gmail password. With 2-Step Verification on, make one at
        Google Account > App passwords (name it "Market digest") and paste the 16 letters.
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    Summaries of what the analysts said and how their calls did, on top of the daily digest:
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Daily time
    
    
    When the digest is made each day. The computer must be on and you must be logged in; a time missed while it was off runs as soon as it is back on.
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
  Extras: shared folder, watchlist, TipRanks and more options
  
    
    
    Shared folder for the Econova hub Optional
    
    
    Each day's digest is also saved as data files, which the Econova dashboard reads. To share them, choose a folder inside Google Drive
      (Google Drive for desktop must be installed). Leave it empty to keep them in the program folder only.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Watchlist Optional
    
    
    A Google Sheet of the stocks you follow, with live prices, adds a price table to the digest. In the sheet, use
      File > Share > Publish to web, choose Comma-separated values (.csv), and paste that link. (USER_GUIDE.md, "Your watchlist", has a ready-made sheet.)
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    TipRanks Optional
    
    
    Adds Wall Street's view (analyst consensus, price targets, earnings dates) for the stocks in the digest. A free key gives 50 look-ups a month;
      the digest uses at most one each weekday. Get one at TipRanks; it starts with tr_live_.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      More options
      
      
      
    After that, each digest covers everything since the last one (up to a week, so days the computer was off are not lost).
    
      
    
      
      
      
    The newest are kept. Gemini's free tier handles about 20 a day.
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
  
    
  
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Ask Claude about your digest Optional
    
    
    If you use the Claude desktop app, connect it and Claude can read your saved videos and analyst calls on this computer. Then ask it
      things like "What did the analysts I follow say about Nvidia this week?" or "Build me a dashboard of this week's calls", or tell it
      to add a channel or an analyst. What Claude reads for an answer is sent to Claude, like anything else you tell it; your keys and passwords never are.
