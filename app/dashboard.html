How to read this page
Invest? Yes / No / Wait
What the analysts' views add up to for that stock: Yes when they clearly favour it, No when they are clearly against it, Wait when they are split. It combines the bullish and bearish calls in your channels' videos (a call counts more from an analyst whose past calls beat the market) with Wall Street's rating from TipRanks. "—" means nobody has given a view yet.
Confidence
How much stands behind the signal, from 5% to 95%: how one-sided the views are, how many calls there are, whether the videos and Wall Street agree, and how earlier calls on that stock turned out.
Analysts
Bullish calls (expect it to rise) against bearish calls (expect it to fall) in the videos.
Wall St
The consensus of professional analysts at banks and brokers, from TipRanks: Strong Buy, Moderate Buy, Hold, Moderate Sell or Strong Sell.
Target
How far above (+) or below (−) today's price those analysts expect the stock in about 12 months.
Smart Score
TipRanks' own 1 to 10 score from eight factors. 8 and above is good; 3 and below is poor.
52-week range
Where today's price sits between the lowest (left) and highest (right) price of the past year.
P/E
Price divided by a year's profit per share. Higher usually means more growth is expected.
Past calls right
A call counts as right when the stock then did better than the S&P 500 (bullish) or worse (bearish).
Earnings
The day a company reports its results. The price often moves a lot that day.

The signals sum up what analysts said; they are not personal financial advice and can be wrong. For decisions that matter, talk to a licensed adviser.
