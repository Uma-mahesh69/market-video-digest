Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Market Digest shows you what analysts are saying about the stocks you follow. This page says what we keep about you and why.

What we keep

  • Your Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture, as Google gives them to us when you sign in.
  • Your list: the stocks you follow, and the number of shares and the price you paid if you choose to type them.
  • Your email choices: whether you want the daily and weekly emails, and the dates we last sent them.

We do not see your Google password, your contacts, or anything else in your Google account. We do not connect to your broker or your bank.

What we use it for

To show your dashboard, to work out your portfolio's value on your screen, and to send you the emails you asked for. Nothing else. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Google Firebase signs you in, stores your list and hosts this website.
  • Our email provider delivers the emails to your address.

The analysis itself (reading videos, prices and analyst ratings) is the same for everyone and uses none of your data.

Your choices

  • See or download it: Account > Download my data.
  • Stop the emails: Account > Emails, or the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Delete it: Account > Delete my account removes your list, holdings and email choices at once.

Cookies

We use only what is needed to keep you signed in and to remember small choices on your device. No tracking or advertising cookies.
